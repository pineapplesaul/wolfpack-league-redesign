· Part Five ·

Rules of the League

Three of them, kept brief on purpose.

❦
I

Attend a race

Every standard adult event counts towards the season. Family and special editions are run purely for the pleasure of it. Your team name is your league identity. Choose it carefully and then live with it.

II

Collect your points

First place receives 100 points, second receives 88, third receives 78, and so on down the order. Every finishing team scores something. Teams that retire gracefully are awarded 10 points for their trouble.

III

Count your best four

Your four finest results decide your final standing. The champions, crowned in October, receive the trophy, the patches, and complimentary entry to every race of Season III. Applause is customary.
